Planning, Design, Modeling & Printing Services

Design from scratch or from a file
Have a ready SVG / STL / STEP / OBJ / 3MF file? Great, send it to us. No file? We'll design from scratch based on a description, an image, or a general idea. If needed, we can also 3D scan with a device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything worth asking

01Why choose NSF3D?▼
NSF3D is a 3D printing studio in Beer Sheva and Southern Israel, specializing in planning, design, and manufacturing custom products. Since launching in early 2026, the studio has provided solutions for private customers, businesses, and institutions — from personal figurines and gifts to functional parts and general-purpose solutions. The studio is run by a mechanical engineer and Ben-Gurion University graduate, with an emphasis on precision, durability, and fully listening to customers' design and engineering needs.
02Can anyone place an order?▼
We serve everyone — private and business customers, institutions, offices, and startups — from a small one-off order to large, repeat orders.
03How much does 3D printing cost?▼
It depends on size, material, and complexity. Prices generally start from ₪40 for small, simple projects, and a custom figurine from ₪800. Either way, you'll get a detailed quote before we begin — no surprises.
04What's the maximum print size?▼
At this stage, our studio can print up to 32×32×32 cm in a single print. Need a larger project? We can print it in separate parts and assemble them — talk to us and we'll find the right solution for you.
05How long does an order take?▼
Most orders are ready within 1-10 business days, depending on workload and project complexity.
06What materials do you print with?▼
We print with a wide range of quality plastics, choosing the material that best suits each project — from PLA for decorative items and figurines, TPU for flexibility, PA for engineering and industrial durability, and more. 👉 See the full materials comparison table
07I don't have a ready file — can you still help?▼
Absolutely! You can send a written description, an image, a sketch, or just a general idea. We'll design the model from scratch and tailor it exactly to your request.
08Can I get a multi-colored print?▼
Yes! Our printers support multi-color printing directly from the printer. Another option is hand-painting with brushes and acrylic paints after printing.
09Which is better — multi-color printing or hand painting?▼
Both methods cost the same. Multi-color printing is more consistent but slower. Hand painting can be faster in some cases and allows for an artistic finish. We'll recommend the best option based on your project and timeline.
10Do you also print in resin?▼
Yes, in addition to standard FDM printing we also print in resin (MSLA) — a technology especially suited to small figurines and fine details that require a high level of precision. Send us a message and we'll advise what's best for your project.
11Do you ship nationwide?▼
Yes. Shipping by courier or post costs around ₪50-120, and orders over ₪1,000 ship free. Self pickup in Beer Sheva — always free.
12How can I pay?▼
Payment in advance — Bit, PayBox, bank transfer, or cash on self pickup.
13What should I do if I have a problem or question?▼
During business hours you can get an immediate response via WhatsApp or phone. Outside those hours, leave a message and we'll get back to you as soon as possible — contact us.
14I changed my mind — can I cancel my order and get a refund?▼
As a business that manufactures custom-made products, once a product has been printed and delivered — the transaction cannot be canceled and no refund can be issued, in accordance with Israel's Consumer Protection Law. Full details in the Terms of Service.
